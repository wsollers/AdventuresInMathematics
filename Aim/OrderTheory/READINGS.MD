# Order Theory Reading Guide

## Reading sequence

1. Davey and Priestley — *Introduction to Lattices and Order*.
2. Grätzer — *Lattice Theory*.
3. Schröder — *Ordered Sets*.

These are the chosen sources for the order-theory development. Editions and
chapter, page, and theorem references will be recorded when the corresponding
material is studied; none are assigned here yet.

## Current scope

Start with preorders and partial orders, alongside the existing total-order
development. Lattice interfaces are deferred for now. Bounds and completeness
proofs can be revisited as they arise in the later real-number constructions.

Keep source-specific definitions and conventions explicit when translating
statements into the project-owned Lean interfaces. Preserve proof stubs for
the learner unless a proof is requested.
